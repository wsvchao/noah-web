About

About the company

Noah Infrastructure LLC, established in 2026, works in the logistics and storage equipment industry. We offer a wide range of racking, shelving and mezzanine solutions for warehouses and commercial storage. We source high-quality products from Asia and deliver cost-effective, reliable solutions to clients across the US.

Noah Infrastructure LLC was established in 2026 to supply racking, shelving, mezzanines and automated storage systems to US warehouses, with engineered layouts, international logistics and project support.

Our Taiwan subsidiary is Ark Infrastructure Technology Co., Ltd. (方舟基建科技有限公司).

Product

Product showroom

Five racking lines, each available in standard sizes or engineered to your building and load data.

Heavy duty

Pallet Racking

Selective racking with direct access to every pallet position. The most common choice for distribution centers.

High density

Drive-in Racking

Forklifts drive into the rack lanes, so you store more pallets of the same product in less floor space.

Floor space

Mezzanines

Steel platforms that add a second working level inside your building, with stairs and safety railings.

Hand-picking

Medium Duty Rack

Shelving for cartons and parts picked by hand, with optional mesh panels for security.

Long loads

Cantilever Racking

Open-front arms for long or bulky items such as boards, pipes and sheet material.
